Privacy Policy

Secure Content Portal is a student project built for an internship screening assignment. It is not a commercial product. This page describes, in plain terms, what happens with your data if you sign in.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google's OAuth service. We store this to create your account and to decide whether you can view content as a Viewer or manage it as an Admin.

What we don't do

We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, or any other Google data beyond your basic profile.

How it's stored

Your account record is stored in a private database used only by this application. Session cookies are HttpOnly and are not accessible to page scripts.

Content you view

If you watch a video or view a PDF hosted on this portal, that activity may be logged (timestamp and item) so the portal's admin can see basic usage statistics. This log is visible only to admins of this portal.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the email listed on the project page.
